== Description ==

 **Top Social Share** is a lightweight and user-friendly WordPress plugin designed to enhance the social sharing experience on your website. With simple integration and customizable buttons, this plugin enables your audience to share your posts effortlessly on popular social platforms like Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIN, Pinterest and WhatsApp.  

### Shortcode: 
Top Social share plugin also provides a shortcode to embed the social sharing buttons in your website content, you can use the ```[top-share]```, and the buttons will appear.

### Key Features:  
- **Social Sharing Buttons:** Add share buttons for Facebook, Twitter, and WhatsApp to your posts and pages.  
- **Customizable Appearance:** Choose button styles, colors, and sizes to match your website design.  
- **Responsive Design:** Buttons are mobile-friendly and look great on all devices.  
- **Simple Setup:** Easily activate and configure the plugin without needing any technical expertise.  
- **Fast and Lightweight:** Optimized for performance to ensure your website stays fast.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I install and activate the Top Social Share plugin? =

- Go to your WordPress admin dashboard.
- Navigate to Plugins > Add New.
- Search for "Top Social Share."
- Click Install Now, then Activate.

= Can I choose where the share buttons appear on my site? =

Yes, you can choose the placement of the share buttons, such as before or after the post content, or use a shortcode to place them manually.

= Are the share buttons responsive and mobile-friendly? =

Absolutely! Top Social Share buttons are designed to be fully responsive and look great on all devices, including mobile and tablets.

= Which social media platforms are supported? =

The plugin currently supports Facebook, Twitter, and WhatsApp. We plan to add more platforms in future updates based on user feedback.

= Can I customize the appearance of the share buttons? =

Yes, you can customize button styles, colors, sizes, and alignments to match your website’s design.

= Does the plugin slow down my website? =

No, Top Social Share is lightweight and optimized for performance to ensure it does not affect your site’s loading speed.

= Is this plugin free? =

Yes, the core version of Top Social Share is free to use. Additional premium features may be introduced in future versions.

= How can I add the share buttons to specific pages or posts? =

You can enable or disable share buttons for individual posts or pages from the post editor or plugin settings.

= Does the plugin support custom post types? =

Yes, Top Social Share can be configured to display buttons on custom post types.

= Who can I contact for support? =

If you have any issues or questions, please visit the support section in the WordPress plugin directory or email us at [contact@buddydevelopers.com].
